Privacy Policy — My Friend

Last updated: November 5, 2026
Data controller: Manuel · hello.myfriendapp@outlook.com

This Privacy Policy explains what information the My Friend app collects, how it is used, where it is stored, and the choices you have. My Friend is a personal finance app that helps you track accounts, transactions, debts, budgets, savings goals, and related financial information.

The app is designed to collect as little personal data as possible and to keep your financial information private to you.

The short version

  • Your financial data is yours. It is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used to profile you.
  • The app contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no crash-reporting.
  • Your data is stored in your own private account and protected so that no other user can read it.
  • Connecting Gmail is optional. If you connect it, My Friend uses read-only access to turn your banks' card-purchase notification emails into transactions for you to review. It cannot send, delete, or change your emails, and you can disconnect at any time.
  • A small number of third-party services help the app function; they are all listed below.
  • You can request access to, or deletion of, your data at any time.

1. Information collected

Account / authentication

To create an account and sign in, the app collects your email address and a password. Your password is handled and stored in hashed form by the authentication provider (Supabase); it is never stored or seen in plain text.

Financial information you enter

The app stores the information you choose to enter: accounts and balances, transactions (amounts, descriptions, notes, dates, categories), statements, debts, payables, budgets, savings goals, investments, subscriptions, and the names or labels of people you track money with (for example, to split an expense). This is stored in your private, access-controlled account so it is available across your devices.

Voice input (optional)

If you use the voice entry feature, the app records a short voice note and converts it to text to pre-fill a transaction for you to review.

  • Transcription: where your device supports on-device speech recognition, the audio is transcribed on your device and does not leave it. Where on-device recognition is unavailable, the audio is processed by Apple's Speech Recognition service.
  • Interpreting the text: the transcribed text is sent to OpenRouter (an AI service) to suggest a structured transaction. To protect your privacy, the app does not send the names of people you track, the identifiers of your accounts, or any of your stored balances or transaction history — only the transcribed text and a list of your category and account names so the suggestion can match your setup. The names of people are matched to your contacts locally on your device.

You always review and confirm a voice-suggested transaction before it is saved. The feature is optional; if you do not use it, no audio or transcript is ever sent anywhere.

Information not collected

The app does not collect analytics, usage tracking, advertising identifiers, location data, or crash reports. It contains no advertising or third-party tracking technology. Unless you choose to connect Gmail (see section 2), the app does not access your email.

2. Gmail connection (optional)

In Settings → Connected Banks you can connect a Gmail account so that card-purchase notification emails from supported banks (currently Banco Popular, BHD and Banco Santa Cruz) show up in the app as transactions waiting for your review. Nothing is added to your accounts or balances until you approve it. This feature is entirely optional; if you never connect Gmail, the app never accesses your email.

What My Friend asks Google for

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly — read-only access to your email, used to read your banks' notification emails.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.labels — used only to create the Gmail labels My Friend watches (BancoPopularSync, BHDSync, BancoSantaCruzSync) if they don't already exist.

With these permissions My Friend cannot send, delete, or change your emails.

How it works

You set up a Gmail filter that applies one of those labels to your bank's notification emails. When a labeled email arrives, Google notifies My Friend's server (through Google Cloud Pub/Sub), and the server fetches the new messages from Gmail, recognizes the supported banks' notifications, and extracts the transaction details. You can also trigger the same check yourself with "Check now". The full email is not saved to the database; only the details listed below are.

What is stored, and where

Everything below is stored in Supabase (the app's database, United States — AWS us-east-1), in tables that only your account (or, where noted, only My Friend's server) can access:

  • Imported transactions: for each approved purchase notification — the bank, amount, currency, merchant, date and time of the transaction, the card's last 4 digits (when the email includes them), the email's subject line, Gmail's internal message ID (so the same email is never imported twice), and its review status (pending, approved, or dismissed). Declined or reversed notifications are not stored. Pending imports are also cached on your device so the app can show them.
  • Your connection: your Gmail address, the encrypted refresh token (see below), and technical details needed to keep the connection running (Gmail's sync position, the IDs of the watched labels, when Gmail's notification "watch" expires, the connection status, and the last error, if any). The app itself cannot read this record; only My Friend's server can.
  • Your ignored cards: the bank and last 4 digits of any card you choose to ignore (see below).

Emails that can't be read

If a bank notification can't be processed (for example, because the bank changed its email format), a troubleshooting record is kept so the problem can be fixed: the Gmail message ID, the time, and an error description. That description can include up to 300 characters of the email's raw content (which may contain the amount, date, merchant, or card's last 4 digits) and the specific values that couldn't be read. These records cannot be read from the app or by other users. They are kept until you ask for them to be deleted or your account is deleted; disconnecting Gmail does not remove them.

Your Gmail access token

When you connect, Google gives My Friend a refresh token that lets its server keep reading new notifications. The token is encrypted (AES-256-GCM) before it is stored, the encryption key is kept separately from the database, and the record cannot be read from the app. The token is never shared with anyone; it is only ever sent back to Google, to read your notifications or to revoke access.

Ignoring specific cards

In Settings → Connected Banks → Ignored cards you can list cards (by bank and last 4 digits) whose notifications you never want imported. When a notification for an ignored card is read, it is skipped before anything is saved, so its transaction is never stored. One exception: if an ignored card's email can't be processed at all, the server can't tell which card it was for, so a troubleshooting record (described above) may still be kept.

Disconnecting

Tapping Disconnect in Settings → Connected Banks:

  • stops Gmail from sending My Friend's server new-email notifications,
  • revokes My Friend's access with Google, so the refresh token stops working, and
  • deletes the connection record, including the encrypted token.

You can also revoke access at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting does not delete transactions that were already imported: approved ones are part of your own records, and pending or dismissed imports and troubleshooting records stay until you ask for them to be deleted.

Deleting imported data

To have your imported email data deleted (pending and dismissed imports, troubleshooting records, your ignored-cards list, and any remaining connection record), email hello.myfriendapp@outlook.com from the address you use to sign in to My Friend. Transactions you already approved are ordinary entries in your accounts; you can delete them in the app, and they are removed along with the rest of your data if you delete your account.

Google API Services User Data Policy

My Friend's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Gmail data is used only to show you your bank transactions for review. It is not used for advertising, not used to train AI or machine-learning models, not sent to OpenRouter or any other AI service, and not sold. It is not transferred to anyone other than the service providers needed to run this feature (Supabase, Vercel and Google, listed in section 4), except where legally required.

3. How your information is used

Your information is used only to provide the core features of the app (storing and displaying your finances across your devices), to authenticate you and keep your account secure, and — where you choose to use them — to interpret voice input into a draft transaction and to import bank notification emails from Gmail for your review. It is not used for advertising or profiling, and is not sold or rented to anyone.

5. How your data is protected

  • Your data is stored in a private account protected by row-level security, so each user can only ever access their own data. This is enforced at the database level and has been tested to confirm one user cannot read another user's information.
  • All communication between the app and its servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
  • If you connect Gmail, your Gmail access token is encrypted before it is stored, and it is never readable from the app (section 2).
  • The app stores a local copy of your data on your device so it works smoothly; this local copy is cleared when you sign out.

6. Data retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account or ask for your data to be deleted, your information is removed from the database. Some information may persist briefly in backups before being overwritten in the normal course of operation.

Gmail connection records are deleted as soon as you disconnect. Imported email transactions and troubleshooting records are kept until you ask for them to be deleted or delete your account (section 2).

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or California's CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. The app does not sell personal information. To exercise any of these rights, contact hello.myfriendapp@outlook.com.

8. Children's privacy

My Friend is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
